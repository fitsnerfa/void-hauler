VOID HAULER

v1.5.0

Every asteroid starts out as plain gray rock — composition unknown. Distress beacons and wreck signals may also appear while you travel: investigate them for rescues and salvage, but beware of pirate traps. Blast one down through its fragments and you won't know what you're actually hauling until the smallest pieces crack open, so a single big rock might hide a mix of common ore and something far more valuable. Scoop up what spills out and haul it back to Ceres Anchorage to sell. Watch out for pirates hunting your cargo hold — fight them off or outrun them. Reinvest in your ship, hire crew, and build a mining operation from a single beat-up hauler.
